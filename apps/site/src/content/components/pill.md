## Preview

```html title="Pill"
<span class="govbb-pill">Information service</span>
<span class="govbb-pill">Digital service</span>
```

The Pill component is a quiet, muted label shown alongside or beneath another
element — for example the service-type label under each service link on the
services index. It is styled as muted body text with a little vertical padding:
no fill, no uppercase and no colour states.

## When to use this component

Use a pill to add a short piece of secondary information to an item, such as
labelling a service as an _Information service_ or a _Digital service_ in a
list of government services. Keep the text short — a couple of words at most —
as pills do not wrap.

## When not to use this component

Do not use a pill for information the user must not miss — it is deliberately
muted and reads as secondary. Do not use it to show the status or phase of a
service, such as _Alpha_ or _Beta_ — use the status banner instead. Do not use
it as a link or a button.
